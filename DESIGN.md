# LUZ Goa — Design System (`DESIGN.md`)

## Visual World & Scene
Built for daytime and evening mobile and desktop use across South Goa (Colomb, Patnem, Palolem). The palette pairs a **Deep Tropical Canopy** shell (`#15241C` / `#1C2E24`) with a high-contrast **Warm Laterite Sand** ground (`#F4F1EB` / `#FAF8F5`) so schedules, time slots, and date ranges remain effortless to scan under bright sunlight.

## Color Tokens
- **Canopy (Primary Ink & Navigation Shell)**:
  - `canopy-950`: `#0F1A14`
  - `canopy-900`: `#15241C`
  - `canopy-800`: `#1C2E24`
- **Sand (Primary Ground & Cards)**:
  - `sand-50`: `#FAF8F5`
  - `sand-100`: `#F4F1EB`
  - `sand-200`: `#EAE4D8`
  - `sand-300`: `#DDD4C3`
- **Semantic Entity & Action Accents**:
  - **One-Time Events & Primary CTA (`terracotta-500`)**: `#D46B54`
  - **Recurring Workshops & Youth Center (`canopy-600`)**: `#325240`
  - **Hourly Services & Highlights (`ochre-500`)**: `#D4AF37`
  - **Property Rentals / Date-Range Stays (`lagoon-600`)**: `#2A6F7B`

## Typography
- **Display / Headings**: `'Frank Ruhl Libre'` (Hebrew) & `'Playfair Display'` (English)
- **UI & Body**: `'Heebo'` (Hebrew) & `'Plus Jakarta Sans'` (English)
- **Numeric Discipline**: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` applied to all dates, time slots, prices, and capacities.
