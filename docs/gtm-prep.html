✅ Visuals & Offerings: At least 1 high-quality photo + minimum 2–3 services/menu items with pricing.

🎤 4. Field Sales Script & Objection Playbook

The 30-Second Walk-In Pitch (Using The Mount Demo):

"Hi! I'm Krutika with our local South Goa Community Platform. We're building a single digital hub that connects tourists, digital nomads, and expats directly with trusted local places here in Canacona, Palolem, and Patnem."

[Open The Mount Demo Page on Phone]

"Look at how The Mount looks here — they get a clean, fast digital storefront with their menu, rates, Google Maps pin, and events. Most importantly, every inquiry goes straight to their WhatsApp with zero commission."

"Since we are launching right now, setting up your business page is 100% free. If you give me 3 minutes, I'll snap two quick photos, take your prices, and put your place live today!"

1. "I already have Instagram and WhatsApp groups." 🔽
Instagram isn't searchable by beach, open hours, or service price, and WhatsApp group messages disappear within an hour.
"Instagram is great for photos, but travelers can't filter it by area, open hours, or price. On our platform, someone looking for yoga or dinner in Palolem right now finds you in 5 seconds and messages your WhatsApp directly."
2. "How much does it cost? Do you take a cut?" 🔽
Trust comes first in Goa: setup is free and we never touch their cash or UPI transactions.
"Zero setup fee, zero commissions on your bookings or cash. Getting listed is 100% free right now. Later on, if you love the customers we bring you, you can choose optional paid featured placement — no pressure at all."
3. "I'm too busy / not good with tech." 🔽
Krutika removes all friction by collecting the info on the spot in 3 minutes.
"You don't need to touch any app or dashboard. I'll handle everything for you right now in 3 minutes — I'll snap two photos, take your price list, and send you the live link on WhatsApp to review."
4. "Who actually uses this platform?" 🔽
Active WhatsApp groups, digital nomads, expat families, and tourists across South Goa.
"Our community ecosystem reaches international travelers, long-stay families, digital nomads, and locals actively looking for curated places across Canacona, Patnem, Palolem, and Agonda."
